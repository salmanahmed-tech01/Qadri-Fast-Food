About Us

Qadri’s Food Mountain is an eclectic restaurant at Dua Chowk in Orangi Town, Karachi. We cook for the neighbourhood, and we keep cooking until 4 AM.

A varied kitchen

Tikka and kabab from the BBQ, karhai and handi, Chinese, pizza and burgers on one menu.

Platters for groups

Couple, friendly, party, family and khandani platters feed 2 to 15 people.
